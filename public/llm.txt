## About This Site
R2G Academy is an elite baseball and softball training facility located in Altamonte Springs, Florida (Seminole County). We offer professional instruction from former MLB players, including Jemile Weeks (Oakland A's, Team USA coach). Our 63' x 26' climate-controlled facility features batting cages, a precision bullpen, and comprehensive training programs for ages 5-18.
